Winding up a fund: our liquidation roadmap

Behind the numbers · 7 September 2026

Liquidating a pension or insurance fund is a precise, regulated process involving the Board, IPEC, administrators and asset managers — with the actuary at the centre of it. It is also a process members experience personally: their benefits are on the line, and the discipline of each step is what protects them.

Here is how a fund moves from board resolution to final deregistration.

The actuary's dissolution valuation sits at the heart of the process: it determines what each member is owed before a single asset is distributed, and it is the document IPEC tests the whole liquidation against. If your fund's board is contemplating a wind-up, involving the actuary early — before the IPEC application — shortens everything that follows.
