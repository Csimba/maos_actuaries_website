What a career here looks like.

Breadth from day one

One team across four disciplines means you see valuations, pricing, risk and reporting — not one cell of a large machine.

Real client exposure

Our flexible outsourcing model puts consultants in front of boards, principal officers and regulators early.

Regional work

Engagements across Zimbabwe, Eswatini, Botswana, South Africa, Malawi and Kenya — different regulators, different markets, faster learning.

Senior mentorship

Work directly with a Resident Actuary with 30+ years of practising experience across the region.

Modern tooling

Our technology advisory practice keeps our own methods current — automation, analytics and clean data pipelines.
