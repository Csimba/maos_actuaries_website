Our methodology

The actuarial control cycle

MAOS applies the actuarial control cycle to every consulting engagement. It is a loop, not a checklist: the solution is monitored, experience is fed back, and the problem is respecified as conditions change — all within the commercial environment, underpinned by professionalism.

Bounded throughout by professionalism
Shaped by the economic, social & regulatory environment
Define the problem

Figure out exactly what needs solving before touching any numbers.

  • A board, employer or regulator has a real question — what should this cover cost? Is the fund solvent?
  • Gather the data and set clear objectives, assumptions and constraints
  • Check the professional and legal context the work must fit inside
Design the solution

Build a model that answers the question, then use it.

  • Build a mathematical or financial model of the problem
  • Run the model to produce numbers: a contribution rate, a reserve, a price
  • Communicate the results clearly to the people who will act on them
Monitor the results

Check reality against the model, and learn from any gap.

  • Compare actual outcomes to what the model predicted
  • Investigate any difference: was the model wrong, or did the world change?
  • Feed the lessons back into how the problem is defined next time
